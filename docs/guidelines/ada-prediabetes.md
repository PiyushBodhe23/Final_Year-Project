# ADA type 2 diabetes prevention

Source: American Diabetes Association Standards of Care, prevention of type 2 diabetes. Public educational summary.

Prediabetes is a laboratory state (for example fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL or HbA1c 5.7–6.4%). It is not a diagnosis that this chatbot can make. People with that pattern often benefit from structured lifestyle change.

The Diabetes Prevention Program pattern is about 7% weight loss if overweight, at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, and a diet lower in refined carbohydrate and sugar-sweetened drinks. These steps lowered progression to diabetes in trials. They do not replace medication decisions made by a doctor.

If fasting glucose is 126 mg/dL or higher, or HbA1c is 6.5% or higher, clinical confirmation is needed. This tool cannot start, stop, or dose diabetes medicines.
