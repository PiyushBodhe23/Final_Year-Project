# AHA heart-health lifestyle

Source: American Heart Association Life's Essential 8 public summary. Educational paraphrase only.

The eight measures are: eat a pattern rich in vegetables, fruit, whole grains, and less sodium; be physically active; do not use tobacco; sleep 7 to 9 hours; manage weight; control cholesterol; control blood sugar; manage blood pressure.

Resting heart rate that is high or rising over weeks is a reason to review fitness, sleep, illness, and blood pressure with a clinician. It is not by itself a diagnosis of heart disease.

Smoking is a major modifiable coronary risk. Stopping smoking is one of the highest-yield lifestyle changes for cardiac risk. Blood pressure at or above 140/90 mmHg needs clinical follow-up, not only wearable coaching.
