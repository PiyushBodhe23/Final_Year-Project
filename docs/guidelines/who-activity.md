# WHO adult physical activity

Source: WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour, 2020. Public summary for this academic prototype.

Adults should do at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity a week, or 75 minutes of vigorous activity, or an equivalent mix. More activity brings extra benefit up to about 300 minutes of moderate activity a week.

Muscle-strengthening should be done on two or more days a week. Long sitting time should be reduced. Daily step counts around 7,000 to 8,000 often sit near this activity level in wearable studies, but the WHO target is minutes of intensity, not steps alone.

This guidance is for apparently healthy adults. It is not a prescription for people with chest pain, fainting, or uncontrolled disease. Those people should speak to a clinician before raising intensity.
