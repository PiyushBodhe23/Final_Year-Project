# Blood pressure and lipids, display ranges

Source: commonly cited adult screening ranges used for display (AHA/ACC and NCEP-style teaching values). These are not personal treatment targets.

Blood pressure below 120/80 mmHg is the usual “normal” display band. 130–139 / 80–89 is elevated or stage 1 in many charts. 140/90 or higher needs clinical review. Home or wearable cuffs can be wrong; clinic confirmation matters.

LDL cholesterol below 100 mg/dL is a common display target for higher-risk adults. HDL above 40 mg/dL in men and 50 mg/dL in women is the usual teaching floor. Triglycerides below 150 mg/dL are the usual display band.

Diet (less trans fat and added sugar), activity, and not smoking support lipid and blood-pressure numbers. Statins, antihypertensives, and doses are clinician decisions only.
