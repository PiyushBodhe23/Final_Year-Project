Why it matters

Health data is everywhere. Understanding is not.

Watches and phones already track how you feel day to day. The hard part is making sense of it before something becomes urgent.

PulseVanguard AI brings those signals together so you are not switching between apps, guessing from one number, or waiting until symptoms are obvious. You get a calm, plain-language picture of risk — shared with the people who care for you when it matters.

Made for real life

Support that feels human, not clinical.

Whether you are watching your own recovery, caring for a parent, or following a patient’s progress, the goal is the same: fewer surprises, earlier conversations.

  • A score you can see through Each Health Risk Index shows which readings raised it, using ranges that shift with age.
  • Plain recommendations Diet tips, rest guidance, and when to seek medical help — based on your vitals and risk level.
  • Family & care visibility Alerts can reach you, a caregiver, and a clinician together, so no one is left guessing alone.
  • A guide you can ask An AI assistant explains your numbers in everyday words — educational support, not a diagnosis.
